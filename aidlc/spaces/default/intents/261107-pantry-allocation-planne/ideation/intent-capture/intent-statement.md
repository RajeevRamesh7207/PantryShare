# Intent Statement

## Problem Statement

- Food spoils in the warehouse while some partner pantries run short. [Q2]

## Target Customer

- The distribution coordinator is the sole decision-maker for what the first version must do. [Q3]

## Success Metrics

- Reduction in spoiled food. [Q1]
  - Unit: percent of received weight that expired before distribution. [Q6]
  - Baseline: calculated from the three months of synthetic distribution history. [Q5]
  - Target: at least 25 percent lower than the baseline. [Q7]
- Fairness of distribution relative to need. [Q1]
  - Definition: each partner's share of allocated food matches its share of total need. [Q8]
  - Baseline: calculated from the three months of synthetic past allocations. [Q9]
  - Target: a partner counts as treated unfairly when its share is more than 20 percent above or below its fair share. [Q10]
- Both measures are judged on each weekly allocation. [Q11]

## Initiative Trigger

- The reason to build now is that food spoils in the warehouse while some pantries run short. [Q2]

## Initial Scope Signal

- Workflow-selected scope: `poc` (workflow-selected). [scope]
- Left out of this first version: individual client tracking, volunteer management, fundraising, delivery route planning, and a connection to the food bank's inventory system. [Q4]
